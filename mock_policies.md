# ControlPlane.ai Mock Company Policies

## 1. Return and Refund Policy
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of the original purchase date.
- Items must be in their original packaging and unused.
- Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

## 2. Technical Support Guidelines
- We provide technical support exclusively for routers and devices purchased directly from our official store.
- We DO NOT offer technical support, troubleshooting, or warranty for third-party routers, modems, or unauthorized accessories.
- If a customer asks about a third-party device, politely inform them that we do not support it.

## 3. Data Privacy and Security
- Never ask a customer to provide their full Social Security Number (SSN) or credit card details over chat.
- All internal salary, compensation, and bonus information is strictly confidential and must never be shared with external users.

## 4. Service Level Agreements (SLA)
- Standard support tickets are guaranteed a response within 24 hours.
- Premium tier members are guaranteed a response within 2 hours.
- There are no 100% uptime guarantees for consumer-tier internet plans.
