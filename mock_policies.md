# ControlPlane.ai Mock Company Policies

## 1. Return and Refund Policy
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of the original purchase date.
- Items must be in their original packaging and unused.
- Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
- Opened software and digital downloads are strictly non-refundable.

## 2. Technical Support Guidelines
- We provide technical support exclusively for routers and devices purchased directly from our official store.
- We DO NOT offer technical support, troubleshooting, or warranty for third-party routers, modems, or unauthorized accessories.
- If a customer asks about a third-party device, politely inform them that we do not support it.
- Escalation to Tier 2 support is only permitted after basic troubleshooting steps have been fully documented in the ticket.

## 3. Data Privacy and Security
- Never ask a customer to provide their full Social Security Number (SSN) or credit card details over chat.
- Customers may only verify their identity using the last 4 digits of their SSN or the last 4 digits of the card on file.
- All internal salary, compensation, and bonus information is strictly confidential and must never be shared with external users.
- Customer databases must never be exported to local developer machines or unauthorized third-party tools.

## 4. Service Level Agreements (SLA)
- Standard support tickets are guaranteed a response within 24 hours.
- Premium tier members are guaranteed a response within 2 hours.
- There are no 100% uptime guarantees for consumer-tier internet plans. Enterprise plans guarantee 99.9% uptime.
- SLA credits for Enterprise plans must be requested by the customer within 30 days of the outage event.

## 5. Employee Conduct and Social Media
- Employees must not disclose upcoming unannounced product features on any public forum or social media platform.
- When posting about the company on personal social media, employees must state that their views do not represent the company.
- Derogatory or offensive language used toward customers or colleagues is grounds for immediate termination.

## 6. Acceptable Use of Company Resources
- Company-issued laptops and mobile devices are for business use only.
- Employees may not install unauthorized third-party software on company devices without IT approval.
- Company email accounts must not be used to register for personal services, newsletters, or online shopping not related to business.

## 7. Travel and Expense Policy
- Daily meal expenses while traveling for business are capped at $75 per day.
- Alcohol purchases will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.
- All expense reports must be submitted within 14 days of returning from a business trip, accompanied by itemized receipts.

## 8. Remote Work Policy
- Remote employees must maintain a secure, private workspace where confidential company information cannot be overseen by non-employees.
- Use of public Wi-Fi without the company VPN is strictly prohibited.
- Core working hours for all remote employees are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which they must be available for meetings.
