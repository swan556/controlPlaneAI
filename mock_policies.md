# Northstar Systems — Customer Policies

## 1. Product Overview
- Northstar Systems provides cloud infrastructure and monitoring software.
- Products include Northstar Cloud, Northstar Edge, Northstar Monitor, and Northstar API.
- Products are available to individual, business, and enterprise customers.
- Product availability varies by region and subscription plan.

## 2. Northstar Cloud
- Northstar Cloud provides managed cloud infrastructure.
- It supports virtual machines, containers, storage, and networking.
- Business plans include automated backups.
- Enterprise plans support custom configurations.
- Northstar Cloud requires a supported operating system and browser.

## 3. Northstar Edge
- Northstar Edge provides infrastructure services closer to customer locations.
- Edge deployments are available only in supported regions.
- Edge features depend on the customer's subscription.
- Hardware-based Edge deployments require compatible Northstar equipment.
- Customers are responsible for maintaining compatible local networking.

## 4. Northstar Monitor
- Northstar Monitor provides infrastructure and application monitoring.
- It supports metrics, logs, alerts, and dashboards.
- Monitoring data can be retained for different periods depending on the plan.
- Alerting is available through email, webhooks, and supported integrations.
- Advanced alerting requires a Business or Enterprise plan.

## 5. Northstar API
- Northstar provides a REST API for supported services.
- API authentication uses API keys or OAuth where supported.
- API requests are subject to plan-specific rate limits.
- API keys must be kept confidential.
- Deprecated API versions are supported for 12 months after deprecation.

## 6. Subscription Plans
### Starter
- $29 per month.
- Up to 5 users.
- 100 GB storage.
- 1 million API requests per month.
- Standard support.

### Business
- $149 per month.
- Up to 25 users.
- 1 TB storage.
- 10 million API requests per month.
- Priority support.

### Enterprise
- Custom pricing.
- Unlimited users.
- Custom storage limits.
- Custom API limits.
- Dedicated support.
- 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.

## 7. Free Trial
- New customers receive a 14-day Business trial.
- No credit card is required for the trial.
- Trial accounts include Business-plan features.
- Trial accounts are limited to 5 users and 100 GB of storage.
- Trial accounts cannot access Enterprise-only features.
- Trial accounts expire automatically after 14 days.

## 8. Subscription Changes
- Customers may upgrade their subscription at any time.
- Upgrades take effect immediately.
- Upgrades are prorated for the remaining billing period.
- Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
- Downgrades may remove features or reduce available limits.
- Customers should reduce usage before downgrading.

## 9. Subscription Cancellation
- Customers may cancel subscriptions from the account dashboard.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- Customers retain access until the subscription expires.
- Annual subscriptions remain active until the end of the paid term.
- Cancellation does not automatically delete customer data.

## 10. Automatic Renewal
- Monthly subscriptions renew every month.
- Annual subscriptions renew every 12 months.
- Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled.
- Customers receive a renewal notice before annual renewal.
- Enterprise renewal terms are defined in the applicable contract.

## 11. Billing
- Monthly plans are billed at the start of each billing period.
- Annual plans are billed annually.
- Northstar accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and ACH for eligible accounts.
- Enterprise customers may pay by invoice.
- Applicable taxes are added to invoices.
- Customers are responsible for maintaining valid payment information.

## 12. Failed Payments
- Failed payments are retried automatically.
- Customers receive an email when payment fails.
- Accounts may be restricted after repeated payment failures.
- Access may be suspended if an outstanding balance remains unpaid.
- Service is restored after successful payment.

## 13. Refunds
- Subscription refunds may be requested within 30 days of the original purchase.
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
- Refunds normally appear within 5–7 business days.
- Annual subscriptions may qualify for prorated refunds when required by the applicable agreement.
- Usage-based charges are non-refundable.
- Free trials are not eligible for refunds.

## 14. Account Management
- Customers may manage users through the organization dashboard.
- Organization owners can add or remove users.
- Business accounts support up to 25 users.
- Enterprise accounts support unlimited users.
- Each user must have a unique account.
- Account ownership transfers require verification.

## 15. User Roles
- Northstar supports Owner, Administrator, Member, and Viewer roles.
- Owners have full organization access.
- Administrators can manage users and organization settings.
- Members can access assigned products and resources.
- Viewers have read-only access.
- Enterprise customers may create custom roles.
