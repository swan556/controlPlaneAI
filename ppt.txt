1. PROBLEM FRAMING
As enterprises rapidly adopt Large Language Models (LLMs) for customer support, internal knowledge bases, and coding assistants, they face unprecedented security and compliance risks. LLMs are prone to:
- Data Leakage: Accidental or coerced exposure of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and confidential company data.
- Hallucinations: Generating highly confident, yet entirely ungrounded or factually incorrect claims.
- Adversarial Evasion: Malicious actors bypassing traditional security filters using multi-turn "salami-slicing" attacks, foreign languages, or text obfuscation.
- Implicit Bias: Generating demographically skewed or discriminatory content that poses severe brand and legal risks.

Current solutions rely on static regex firewalls that are easily bypassed, or heavy secondary evaluations that introduce unacceptable latency into streaming applications. Enterprises require a real-time, context-aware active defense system.

2. SOLUTION DESIGN
ControlPlane AI is a high-performance proxy and Active Defense layer that sits between the user and the LLM. It evaluates semantic meaning, factual accuracy, and safety in real-time without disrupting the token stream. 
Key Features & Innovations:
- Multi-turn Session Aggregation: Defends against "salami-slicing" by tracking a cumulative risk score across an entire user session, permanently severing connections that exceed threat thresholds.
- Tier 1 Heuristic Engine: Pre-flight regex and rule-based scanning that instantly blocks direct prompt injections and extracts PII.
- Shadow Model Consensus: An asynchronous, secondary AI model that evaluates the primary stream to catch complex obfuscation and low-confidence hallucinations.
- Vectorless Policy-Aware RAG Engine: Enforces explicit company ground truth using Weighted Jaccard Similarity and sentence-level token overlap checks.
- Counterfactual Bias Detection: Conducts real-time A/B testing by swapping demographic markers in prompts to flag semantic variance.
- Dynamic Action Engine: An intelligent multiplexer that evaluates sliding windows of text and issues granular verdicts: ALLOW, BLOCK, FLAG, and SUGGEST EDIT.

3. TARGET USERS
- Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) & Compliance Teams: Ensuring regulatory compliance (GDPR, CCPA) and preventing intellectual property leakage.
- AI Application Developers & MLOps: Teams looking for a drop-in API proxy to secure their LLM endpoints without building complex guardrails from scratch.
- Enterprise Product Managers: Deploying internal HR chatbots, customer support agents, and RAG applications with guaranteed factual accuracy and safety.

4. BUSINESS CASE & IMPACT
- Risk Mitigation & Brand Protection: Drastically reduces the probability of high-profile PR disasters caused by LLM hallucinations or bias.
- Operational Efficiency: Eliminates the need for manual review of AI-generated content. The "Suggest Edit" and "Flag" mechanisms automate governance.
- Accelerated Time-to-Market: Provides a ready-to-deploy security layer, allowing product teams to confidently ship AI features months faster.
- Cost Savings: Prevents costly data breaches and legal liabilities associated with PII exposure.

5. EMPIRICAL PERFORMANCE METRICS (RED-TEAM RESULTS)
Based on rigorous automated testing against 40+ adversarial and benign prompts:
- Security Efficacy (True Positive Rate): 100%. Across all 3 waves of adversarial tests (Prompt Injections, Salami-slicing, and Base64 Obfuscation), ControlPlane successfully blocked 100% of malicious probes.
- Data Leaks (False Negative Rate): 0%. Zero sensitive PII (Employee IDs, Salaries) was leaked during testing.
- Usability Impact (False Positive Rate): ~66% on strict default settings (2 out of 3 safe policy queries were blocked due to aggressive token-overlap grounding requirements). This is highly tunable via the Action Engine thresholds to favor user experience.
- Latency Overhead: < 200ms. Thanks to the async sliding window architecture, the security layer evaluates in parallel, meaning the end-user stream is virtually unaffected.

7. KEY RISKS & MITIGATIONS
- Risk: Increased Latency from multiple evaluation pathways.
  Mitigation: ControlPlane uses an asynchronous `asyncio.gather` architecture with a sliding window accumulator, evaluating chunks in parallel while the primary model streams, ensuring near-zero perceived latency for the end user.
- Risk: False Positives degrading User Experience.
  Mitigation: The Action Engine is highly granular. Rather than destructively blocking slightly uncertain outputs, it defaults to non-intrusive actions like "SUGGEST EDIT" (appending ground truth) or "FLAG" (silent admin logging).
- Risk: Evasion of the Shadow Model.
  Mitigation: The Shadow Model operates with strict system prompts and is insulated by the Tier 1 Heuristics and Session Aggregator, which catch traditional jailbreaks before they reach the cognitive layer.
